The production process used for manufacturing our screws offers significant structural advantages. Thanks to hot forging, the longitudinal continuity of the fibers is preserved, even in the critical transition area between the head and the shank. This ensures greater mechanical strength and component reliability. Moreover, this technology allows for a considerable saving of raw material, positively impacting both costs and the overall sustainability of the process. The thread is formed through rolling, preserving the continuity of the fibers across the entire surface of the part and thus preventing the formation of notches that could act as initiation points for fracture.
